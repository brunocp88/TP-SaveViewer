Projects
Start uses the project's stored created_week when available. Complete shows the recorded finish week for completed projects, or an estimated finish week for ongoing design-phase projects calculated from stored design duration/progress and the current staff-to-baseline-staff ratio. Completed projects generally do not preserve their original start week in a single current save, so those cells are shown as unavailable rather than inferred. Performance gains and Lap delta use the exact stored project/design projection when available; if an ongoing concept has not generated a projection yet, those cells are left neutral. Ongoing projects preserve both the chosen budget and current spend. Completed chassis designs often preserve the chosen project budget, but completed aero-history entries generally do not preserve their original project spend, so those cells remain unavailable instead of being inferred. The Summary view centralizes season-to-date cumulative development gains and team development-finance information.
