# TP Save Lab v0.58: what was added since v0.54 (condensed)

File: `TP_Save_Lab_v0.58.html`

## New tabs and features
- **Schedule:** schedule generator, for current and next season. Load tracks .json files and edit/create schedules within the Lab.
- **Create and Edit Suppliers:** upload your engines.json, parts.json and tyre_suppliers.json, and create/edit/delete engines/parts/tyre suppliers.
- **Regulations:** change regulations, create new regulations options (no json upload necessary) where applicable.
- **Load Another Save:**  load another save, re-loading a save without needing to refresh the page.

## Drivers
- **Contracts:** seats share each team row's width equally (halves, thirds or quarters by entries); junior teams always show three seats; click or drag a driver into an empty seat to move the contract, drag onto another driver to swap (short confirmation, undoable); injury marker on seats; EDIT DRIVER button in the driver panel.
- **Driver status pairing:** a team's two main drivers are Equal + Equal or No.1 + No.2; changing one makes the other follow; Save Health warns about clashes.
- **Driver editor:** Create driver works with the new `personalities` field.
- **Database:** filters (Contracted only, Free agents only, Generated drivers only / ≤18, ≤21, ≤23).
- **Negotiations:** "Hide reserve" filter for drivers; sortable Role column and per-role filter buttons for staff.

## Championship
- **Standings:** driver table keeps its width across seasons; junior series have a Season selector with past seasons and their Season overview.
- **Export this page:** Full CSV and PNG image of the visible series and season, season overview included.
- **Results:** improved logic for race abbreviations for tracks MonteCarlo, MagnyCours, Estoril.

## Regulations and rules
- **Regulations tab:** current rules editable, no header, Engine rules row (week 41), era severity under Next season, **+ Add option** on rules with numbers or lists (starts from a similar option with different values, editable key, duplicate key blocked; confirmed in the game with the 150 kg tank).
- **Edits and events:** full editor for the Engine regulation change event (chance, up-to-roll limits, power threshold).

## Money and cars
- **Finances:** collapsible windows; All income shows its Week 52 estimate correctly (finally!); HQ upgrades tooltip names the exact upgrade.
- **Warehouse:** unassigned parts hidden; base chassis shows Baseline; year-old chassis shows its −5 malus.
- **Projects and cars:** car baseline and next-year chassis editors use red − / green + steppers and show the change since the save was loaded, kept after Apply.
- **Technology tab** (was Electronics): all teams in one grid, 10 driver aids and ERS, allowed/banned by the save's rules.
- **Suppliers > Performance:** Active only on by default; Tyres table with Grip, Durability and Temperature (Soft/Medium/Hard) and a Development and prices view; tyre supplier editor (grip, durability, temperature, leave chance).

## General
- Row hover on data tables; clicking the TP Save Lab logo restarts the app (asks first with unsaved edits); landing page links to TP Stint Lab and TP Finance Lab.
